# Elite Smiles Instagram Editorial Profile

Source reviewed: the public `@elitesmilesutah` profile in Chrome, including the visible post grid and the supplied full-post reference.

## Visual signature

- Default format: 4:5 Instagram feed creative.
- Palette: creamy ivory, warm white, charcoal, black, and restrained champagne-gold.
- Typography: elegant serif display headlines with clean sans-serif support copy.
- Lighting: soft daylight, polished portrait lighting, gentle contrast, premium but approachable.
- Photography: confident women and men, natural expressions, close-up smiles, lifestyle confidence, and carefully framed before/after cases.
- Composition: magazine/editorial layouts, strong whitespace, clear hierarchy, one main idea per post.

## Recurring creative families

1. Treatment education cards with a large headline and benefit checklist.
2. Premium portrait posts connecting smiles to confidence and everyday life.
3. Close-up smile and lip details for visual attention.
4. Before/after transformations with restrained labels and consistent framing.
5. Dark charcoal luxury panels with gold/ivory type for premium treatment offers.
6. Consultation-led posts with location, financing qualification, and a clear CTA.

## Copy pattern

Posts commonly move through:

1. Emotional or benefit-led headline.
2. Plain-language explanation of the treatment or outcome.
3. Short benefit list.
4. Draper, Utah location context.
5. Complimentary consultation CTA.
6. Service-specific hashtags.

## Image-generation rules

Nano Banana creates the clean visual base only. It must not render logos, brand marks, watermarks, or typography. It should leave a deliberate clean zone for a separate text-layout layer so the finished creative can match the account’s editorial cards without relying on unreliable image-model text.

Generated images should feel like the current feed: premium, warm, human, realistic, and dental-anatomy accurate. Avoid neon colors, loud gradients, generic dental stock scenes, exaggerated whitening, distorted teeth, clutter, and artificial-looking faces.
