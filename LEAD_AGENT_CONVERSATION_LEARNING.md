# Lead Agent conversation learning

On 2026-09-24, 30 CRM leads with a recorded consultation date were reviewed through the read-only CRM API. The sample includes booked, completed, no-show/reschedule, treatment-completed, and later-nurtured leads. This is an operational review, not a causal experiment: an appointment date does not establish attendance or prove that each message in a thread was effective.

Before the recorded visit, 26/30 had inbound SMS or email; 14/30 had an inbound SMS question; 20/30 mentioned a day or time preference. These are approximate keyword counts. A small, unmatched sample of 15 recent nurture leads with inbound messages and no consultation date had 4/15 questions and 1/15 day/time mention. The cohorts differ in age and follow-up duration, so the comparison does not measure conversion lift.

Generalized guidance now used by the Agent: answer the patient's practical question before offering a visit; keep the patient's language; acknowledge their goal and any existing scheduling preference; have Rod verify live availability before presenting times; and send one accurate confirmation after acceptance. Clinical and treatment-cost questions require Rod.

The review also found mistakes in threads that eventually booked: duplicate nudges, language switches, premature DOB requests, unsupported availability, and inconsistent reminder times. Those must not be copied. No raw patient messages from the review are stored as reusable examples. Existing touchpoint outcome rankings can continue to inform strategy selection, but outcomes are correlations and do not override safety or patient intent.
